How to test

  1. Open SaoPiao on an iPhone and allow camera access. (The ad-tracking prompt may appear first; either choice is fine.)
  2. On the Scan tab, choose 10 張 (10 invoices) and tap 開始掃描 (Start scanning).
  3. Point the camera at Sample A below (both QR codes in view). The counter shows 1 / 10 with a “已加入” (added) message.
  4. Point the camera at Sample A again — the app shows “已掃過” (already scanned); duplicates are not added.
  5. Point the camera at Sample B with both QR codes in view. The app merges the right QR code, so all 6 items are recorded.
  6. Tap 完成 (Done). The batch preview lists both invoices with totals.
  7. Tap the share icon → CSV or Excel (XLSX) to open the iOS share sheet.

The seller tax ID in these samples is fictitious, so the store-name lookup returns no result and the app shows the tax ID instead. With real invoices, the store name and address are filled in from Taiwan’s Ministry of Finance open data.
